## 🔘 Post Types & Interactive Buttons

When a creator you follow posts a new update, the bot will send you a message. Below the message, AiSocialFeed provides intelligent, context-aware buttons based on exactly what kind of post it is (Text, Image, or Video) and your current subscription plan.

### 📝 Text & Image Posts
For standard text posts, articles, or image galleries, you will see buttons to help you process the information quickly:
- **[ 🤖 Summary ]**: (Premium) Tap this, and the AI will condense long articles or threads into a quick 2-3 sentence summary.
- **[ 🌐 Translate ]**: (Premium) Tap this to instantly translate the post into your native language (supports 330+ languages).
- **[ 🔗 View ]**: Opens the original post in your browser.
- **[ 🔖 Save ]**: Bookmark the post to read it later.

### 🎬 Video & Audio Posts
When a post contains a video or audio track (like a YouTube video, TikTok, or Instagram Reel), the bot provides special media buttons:
- **[ ⬇️ Link ]**: (Pro/Premium) Generates a direct download link for the video in different qualities (480p, 720p, or 1080p).
- **[ 📥 File ]**: (Premium) Directly uploads the video file (up to 480p) straight into your Telegram chat so you don't even have to leave the app.
- **[ 🎵 Audio ]**: (Pro/Premium) Extracts just the audio track. Pro users get an audio download link, while Premium users receive the audio file directly in chat.
- *(Plus all standard buttons like View, Summary, Translate, and Save)*
